About the

Event

Inspired by the elegance and grandeur of the British Regency Era, THE ROYAL WALK OF HONOR transforms REMAX’s 12th Anniversary into an extraordinary royal celebration, where its valued dealers take center stage as the Kings and Queens of the evening. Their contributions, achievements, and lasting partnerships deserve more than recognition they deserve a moment of royal honor.

As REMAX celebrates 12 years of success, this special evening is dedicated to celebrating the people behind the legacy, recognizing their remarkable achievements, and expressing appreciation for their continued trust and partnership.

From majestic royal entrances and enchanting ballroom experiences to distinguished award presentations, every moment is designed to make each dealer feel truly valued, celebrated, and treated like royalty.

Because tonight, the crown belongs to them, the spotlight is theirs, and every achievement deserves a royal celebration.
